The symbols, in plain language

b · Baseline pace
Underlying pace before race-day variation, in s/km.
e · Race-day effect
An offset in s/km: negative means faster, positive means slower.
p · Predicted race pace
Baseline plus race-day effect: p = b + e, in s/km.
yⱼ · Observed trial pace
The j-th measurement, in s/km.
T · Predicted finish time
Pace multiplied by 10 km, giving seconds.

Normal distributions above use mean and standard deviation, both in s/km. Labels state the model assumptions; coloured curves show the selected distribution view.

About this example

This is an illustrative example of the project’s ideas. Predictions use synthetic data; the conversation and tool activity are simulated. The training scenario’s 65% probability is an assumed value, not an estimated causal effect. No personal athlete data or validated performance claims are presented.
